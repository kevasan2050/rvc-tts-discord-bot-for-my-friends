Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this Discord bot ("the Bot") processes information when you use it.

1. Information Processed

When you interact with the Bot, it may process information necessary to provide its functionality, including:

• text submitted through Bot commands;
• Discord user, server, channel, and interaction identifiers or metadata provided by Discord;
• information required to connect to and operate within Discord voice channels.

2. Text-to-Speech and Voice Processing

Text submitted to text-to-speech commands is processed for the purpose of generating synthesized speech.

The generated audio may also be processed through voice conversion software before being played in a Discord voice channel.

Text or audio may be transmitted to third-party services when required to provide text-to-speech or other functionality.

3. Temporary Files

The Bot may create temporary audio files while processing text-to-speech and voice conversion requests.

These files are used only as necessary to provide the requested functionality and are automatically removed during normal operation. They are not intended to be retained as a permanent archive of user requests.

4. Data Storage

The Bot does not intentionally maintain a permanent database containing the contents of users' text-to-speech requests.

Technical logs may temporarily contain information necessary for operation, debugging, or error diagnosis.

5. Data Sharing

The Bot does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services when necessary for the Bot's operation. Those services process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Discord

The Bot operates through Discord and therefore also depends on information provided by Discord. Use of Discord is subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to limit unnecessary storage and access to information processed by the Bot. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the Bot's functionality or data processing practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will indicate the latest revision.
